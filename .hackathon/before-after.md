# Before / After Control Experiment

**User Task:** 
"Received current offer $120k for CloudForge renewal. Give strategy."

### Mode: Control (No Hindsight Memory)
- **Recommendation:** Standard counter-offer: request a 5-10% discount.
- **Reasoning:** Without historical context, 5-10% is a safe industry average for software renewals.
- **Evidence Used:** None

### Mode: Hindsight (Memory Active)
- **Recommendation:** Reject the initial offer. Mention Azure and hold out for a 15% discount in late December.
- **Reasoning:** CloudForge has a distinct pattern of escalating discounts to 15% in the final week of Q4 when competitive pressure is applied.
- **Evidence Used:** 
  1. Quote #101: $100k annual
  2. Email: We can't do $100k, evaluating Azure.
  3. Email: I can drop to $85k if we close by EOY.
  4. Contract signed for $85k.
  5. Quote #205: $110k annual renewal
  6. Email: Azure offered $90k.
  7. Email: My VP approved $93.5k (15% off) to match, must sign by Dec 31.
  8. Contract signed for $93.5k.
